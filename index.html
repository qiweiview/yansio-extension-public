Privacy Policy for 言修 (Yansio)

Last updated: October 17, 2026

言修 (Yansio) is a browser extension that helps improve, rewrite, or translate text when you ask it to. This policy explains what information the extension uses and how it is handled.

Information the extension uses

  • Text and website content: Text you select or submit for processing, and nearby writing context needed to provide an in-page suggestion. The extension accesses page content on supported pages when you use its feature.
  • Personal communications: Selected email, message, or other communication text is treated as website content when you choose to process it.
  • Authentication information: If you configure an API key or other credential for an AI service, the extension stores it with your extension settings in your browser profile and uses it to authorize requests to the service you configure.
  • Web history / page context: The active page address and site context may be read when you invoke the extension, to apply your site-specific rules. The extension does not need to collect a browsing history from your account.
  • Settings and history: Model configuration, endpoint, site rules, privacy preferences, and any enabled local history are stored in your browser profile.

How information is used and shared

When you request a rewrite, translation, or other AI-assisted operation, the text you chose and the context needed for that request are sent to the AI provider and endpoint configured in the extension, together with the credential needed to authorize the request. The provider processes that information under its own privacy policy and terms. Review those terms before configuring a provider. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

言修 does not sell your personal information. It uses the information described above to provide its writing-assistance features and the settings you choose. Settings and optional history remain in your browser profile unless you configure a service that receives information as described above.

Data retention and deletion

Settings, credentials, site rules, and any enabled local history remain in your browser profile until you remove them or uninstall the extension. You can edit or clear settings and history in the extension interface. Information sent to an AI provider may be retained by that provider under its own policy.

Security

Use a trusted AI provider and protect access to your browser profile and any configured API key. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

言修 is a general-purpose writing tool and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. The revision date above indicates the latest update.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the extension publisher through the Chrome Web Store listing for 言修 (Yansio).

言修 (Yansio) 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 17 日

言修是一款浏览器扩展，可在你主动使用时帮助改进、改写或翻译文字。本政策说明扩展使用哪些信息以及如何处理这些信息。

扩展使用的信息

  • 文字和网站内容：你选择或提交处理的文字，以及生成页面内建议所需的附近写作上下文。你使用功能时，扩展会在支持的网页上读取页面内容。
  • 个人通信：你主动选择处理的邮件、消息或其他通信内容，按网站内容处理。
  • 身份验证信息：如果你配置了 AI 服务的 API 密钥或其他凭据，扩展会将其与扩展设置一起保存在浏览器配置文件中，并用于向你配置的服务授权请求。
  • 网页记录／页面上下文：你触发扩展时，扩展可能读取当前页面地址和站点上下文，以应用你设置的站点规则。扩展无需收集你账户中的完整浏览历史。
  • 设置和历史记录：模型配置、服务端点、站点规则、隐私偏好以及启用的本地历史记录保存在浏览器配置文件中。

信息的使用和共享

当你请求改写、翻译或其他 AI 辅助操作时，你选择的文字及该请求所需的上下文，会连同授权请求所需的凭据一起发送到扩展中配置的 AI 提供方和服务端点。提供方依据其自身的隐私政策和服务条款处理这些信息。配置服务前请阅读其条款；请勿提交你无权分享的信息。

言修不会出售你的个人信息。扩展将上述信息用于提供写作辅助功能及你选择的设置。扩展设置和可选历史记录保存在浏览器配置文件中；如果你配置了会接收信息的服务，则按上文说明处理。

保存期限和删除

设置、凭据、站点规则以及启用的本地历史记录会保留在浏览器配置文件中，直到你删除它们或卸载扩展。你可以在扩展界面修改或清除设置和历史记录。发送给 AI 提供方的信息可能由该提供方按照其自身政策保存。

安全

请使用可信的 AI 提供方，并妥善保护浏览器配置文件及已配置的 API 密钥。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

儿童

言修是一款通用写作工具，并非专为儿童设计。

政策变更

当扩展功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，本政策可能更新。上方日期表示最近更新时间。

联系

如有隐私问题，请通过 Chrome Web Store 上的言修 (Yansio) 扩展商店页面联系发布者。
